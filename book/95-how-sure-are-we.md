# How sure are we?

This book asks you to say how sure you are, what that rests on and what would change your mind. Here is its own answer, claim by claim. A *published finding* has a study behind it, and its chapter says where the study stops. *Common practice* is what careful people have long done, untested here. *Our bet* is this book's reasoning. *High*, *medium* and *low* are the book's own judgment.

## How to argue with this book

Choose a row and send it the second question: what does it rest on, and where does it stop? Then test it on your own record: your recordings, the sentences people said back, the dates you gave and what became of them. If a month of the practice moves nothing there, the claim has failed for you, whatever its label says. Keep what moved.

You have one more reason to check. An AI assistant drafted this manuscript, and its author directed and checked the work. A fluent page suggests little about what stands behind it, and this is a fluent book. Most of its studies were read as published abstracts, and whoever checks a machine's draft can miss what it missed.

A checked counterexample outranks any label in that table. Show the author a record, a date or a failed repeat, and the claim will change.
