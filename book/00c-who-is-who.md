# Who's who

Every person and company here is invented. Each chapter says who people are when you meet them; this page is for reading out of order.

- **Lucía**, co-founder and chief executive of Tolva, a small company in Montevideo. Its software sends trucks to warehouse doors.
- **Mateo**, co-founder, runs technology and is also the architect.
- **Bianca**, product manager. **Tyler**, who leads delivery. **Diego**, from Costa Rica, the engineer placed with the client in Rotterdam.
- **Claire**, who is French and runs operations at Veldhaven, a food wholesaler in Rotterdam and Tolva's largest client. **Otávio**, who heads its information security. **Wesley**, who runs its procurement.
- **Naomi**, a data consultant in Seattle who works alone. Veldhaven and Sarnik both buy her forecast of truck arrivals.
- **Hugo**, who runs operations at Sarnik, a chilled and frozen food distributor near Lyon.
- **Renata**, Tolva's first investor, in São Paulo.

The story runs from March to November. Veldhaven has three warehouses to move onto Tolva's scheduler before its peak in December.
