# Notas

Cada *La investigación dice* y cada *Mito a prueba* de este libro se apoya en un estudio publicado. Estos son los títulos, con la revista y el año, capítulo por capítulo, para que usted pueda buscar uno y hacerle la segunda pregunta. Cada recuadro dice hasta dónde llega su estudio. Tres estudios quedan fuera de la lista por decisión del autor: el que trata sobre qué tan bien entiende la gente los aparatos (*Empiece aquí*, *Sepa lo que piensa*, *La explicación*) y los dos que están detrás del *Mito a prueba* de *Voz, cuerpo y cámara*.

- *Empiece aquí*: "Speakers' overestimation of their effectiveness," *Psychological Science*, 2002.
- *La palabra exacta*: "Consequences of erudite vernacular utilized irrespective of necessity: Problems with using long words needlessly," *Applied Cognitive Psychology*, 2006.
- *¿Qué tanta seguridad tiene? Dígalo*: "Do people inherently dislike uncertain advice?" *Psychological Science*, 2018; "Exploring the 'planning fallacy': Why people underestimate their task completion times," *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 1994.
- *Con una máquina en la sala* (Parte I): "Automation bias and errors: Are crews better than individuals?" *International Journal of Aviation Psychology*, 2000; "Complacency and bias in human use of automation: An attentional integration," *Human Factors*, 2010.
- *Pensar sobre la marcha*: "Slow lies: Response delays promote perceptions of insincerity," *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 2021.
- *Una escucha que se siente*: "Changes in medical errors after implementation of a handoff program," *New England Journal of Medicine*, 2014.
- *Preguntas y terreno común*: "Do conversations end when people want them to?" *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, 2021.
- *La reunión*: "The impact of leader eye gaze on disparity in member influence: Implications for process and performance in diverse groups," *Academy of Management Journal*, 2021.
- *El avance*: "Do you want the good news or the bad news first? The nature and consequences of news order preferences," *Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin*, 2014.
- *La demo*: "Service recovery paradox: A meta-analysis," *Journal of Service Research*, 2007; "A longitudinal study of complaining customers' evaluations of multiple service failures and recovery efforts," *Journal of Marketing*, 2002.
- *El pitch*: "When good evidence goes bad: The weak evidence effect in judgment and decision-making," *Cognition*, 2011.
- *La venta*: "If you need help, just ask: Underestimating compliance with direct requests for help," *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 2008.
- *El encargo*: "The expertise reversal effect," *Educational Psychologist*, 2003; "Accountability and automation bias," *International Journal of Human-Computer Studies*, 2000.
- *Por escrito*: "Egocentrism over e-mail: Can we communicate as well as we think?" *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 2005.
- *Entre fronteras*: "The extreme illusion of understanding," *Journal of Experimental Psychology: General*, 2022.
- *La conversación difícil*: "Do you want the good news or the bad news first? The nature and consequences of news order preferences," *Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin*, 2014.
- *Discutir bien*: "When good evidence goes bad: The weak evidence effect in judgment and decision-making," *Cognition*, 2011.

- *La presión y cómo resistirla*: "Dispelling the illusion of invulnerability: The motivations and mechanisms of resistance to persuasion," *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 2002; "The truth about the truth: A meta-analytic review of the truth effect," *Personality and Social Psychology Review*, 2010.

- *Ciencia de datos*: "Algorithm aversion: People erroneously avoid algorithms after seeing them err," *Journal of Experimental Psychology: General*, 2015; "Automation bias and errors: Are crews better than individuals?" *International Journal of Aviation Psychology*, 2000; "Complacency and bias in human use of automation: An attentional integration," *Human Factors*, 2010.
- *Fundadores y la startup en la era de la IA*: "Automation bias and errors: Are crews better than individuals?" *International Journal of Aviation Psychology*, 2000; "Complacency and bias in human use of automation: An attentional integration," *Human Factors*, 2010.

La mayoría de estos estudios se leyó por sus resúmenes, y los recuadros no afirman más de lo que esos resúmenes dicen. Ninguno se hizo por encargo para este libro, y cualquiera de ellos puede resultar equivocado. Si eso pasa, el consejo se sostiene o se cae con el razonamiento que lo rodea.
