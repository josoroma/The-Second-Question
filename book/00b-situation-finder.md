# Find your moment

Find the row closest to your moment, open that chapter at the practice named there, and do that first; the rest of the chapter can wait.

**None of these fits.** Go to *Know what you think* and do *ask it before they do*: the first line you can't fill shows you what to work on next.

**It isn't a wording problem.** Harassment, discrimination, threats, retaliation, a risk to someone's safety, anything unlawful: these have no row here and no script in this book. Read *What is not a wording problem*, the last section of *Difficult people*.
